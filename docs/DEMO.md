# A vehicle's complete journey

For an existing installation, see [account migration notes](ROLES.md); existing staff login emails stay the same.

Open `storage/app/private/demo-accounts.txt` on this computer. It contains locally generated passwords for `admin@transolutions.test`, `secretary@transolutions.test`, `mechanic@transolutions.test`, `purchaser@transolutions.test`, and `customer@transolutions.test`. Each account must choose a new password on its first login. Demo addresses do not receive real email.

## Already-completed example

1. Sign in as Admin and change the temporary password.
2. Open Bookings → DAV 2105 (Suzuki Ertiga). Its stepper ends at Paid / Released.
3. Open its invoice: the service and Transmission Filter line were generated by completion; GCash payment was recorded by the secretary.
4. Open Vehicle service history to see diagnosis, parts, mechanic, and the paid amount.
5. Open Reports for the seed date. The payment appears in daily, weekly, and monthly sales. Export CSV or PDF.

## Walk through a new repair

1. **Purchaser:** sign in, change password, open Parts Orders → New parts order. Select Transmission Filter, quantity 3, and unit cost PHP 275. Save draft → Mark as ordered. On Receive Stock, receive all items. Inventory increases by 3 and a stock-in ledger entry appears. A second receipt is blocked.
2. **Customer:** sign in, change password, open Book a Check-up. Choose DAV 2101 (Toyota Vios), Inspection, and tomorrow at 9:00 AM. Enter “Transmission slips during gear changes.” Optionally request pick-up. Submit. The booking is Pending.
3. **Secretary:** sign in, change password, open this booking. Confirm tomorrow at 9:00 AM → Next step: Schedule. It appears in the daily schedule. Seed day already has four confirmed jobs, so choose another date for the demonstration.
4. **Secretary:** select Marco Reyes → Next step: Start job. Status becomes Processing. Sign in as **Mechanic (Marco Reyes)** and add diagnosis: “Replace the transmission filter, inspect fluid, and road test.” Save.
5. **Customer:** open My Bookings → this booking. Read the diagnosis and estimate, then Agree to repair. Declining instead pauses parts usage and completion; the mechanic can revise diagnosis to request a new decision.
6. **Mechanic:** select Transmission Filter and quantity 1 → Record parts used. Inventory decreases by 1, and the same part appears in the job. Trying a quantity greater than available stock is rejected without changing stock.
7. **Mechanic:** Next step: Complete. The invoice is generated and the customer receives an in-app “vehicle ready” notification.
8. **Secretary:** View invoice → select Cash, Bank Transfer, GCash, or PayMaya → Mark paid & release vehicle → confirm. Status becomes Paid / Released. Print the invoice.
9. **Customer:** open My Vehicles → Service history and My Invoices. The complete repair appears without re-encoding.
10. **Admin:** open Reports for today to see the payment, booking status, used part, and activity log. Only Admin can use Owner controls on a booking to reverse a step with a reason.

Dates use Asia/Manila; currency is PHP. Payment recording confirms money received outside the system; GCash and PayMaya are payment-method labels, not payment gateway integrations. The invoice is an internal shop billing document; tax registration fields and statutory invoicing configuration need the shop's actual business details.
